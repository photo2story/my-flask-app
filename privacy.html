This application may collect non-personal data such as:

2. Data Usage

Collected data is used solely to:

We share data only with service providers required to operate the app (e.g., Firebase/Google OAuth). We do not sell personal data.

All portfolio and simulation data is stored securely using Firebase services. Authentication is handled by Google OAuth.

This application is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
