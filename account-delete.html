Account Deletion

This page explains how to request deletion of your Beat SnP account and associated data.

How to request deletion

Email us at: photo2happy@gmail.com

Please include:

  • The email address used to sign in to Beat SnP
  • Optional: your display name (nickname)

What will be deleted

  • Your account record (email, display name, profile photo URL if stored)
  • Your saved portfolios and simulation settings stored in Firebase/Firestore

What may be retained

  • Basic records required for legal/security purposes (if any), for the minimum time necessary

Processing time

We will delete the data within 7 days after verifying the request.

Contact

Questions? Email photo2happy@gmail.com.
